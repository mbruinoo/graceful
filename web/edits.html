Pick an edit, find the numbering

Start from the tree, not the line. Choose an edge to unhook and two dots to hook it between. The page looks for a way to number the dots so that your edit is one slide of one bracket.

Try it

Two kinds of edit

Both ends move

The new edge shares no dot with the old one. A slide does exactly this: each foot goes to the next dot along the line. So you need a numbering where the two new ends carry the numbers just after (or just before) the two old ends.

This almost always exists.

One end stays (re-hang)

You keep one end and move the other, like moving a twig to a different branch. A slide moves both feet, so the only bracket that can do this is the shortest one, pivoting: the kept dot must sit between the old and new neighbours, with three numbers in a row.

This is much rarer.

What this says about the puzzle

For the edit you have in mind there is usually a numbering that makes it a single step. The catch is the same as in a sliding-car puzzle: you are not free to renumber. Getting from the numbering you have to the one you need is itself a chain of slides, and whether that chain always exists is the open question.
