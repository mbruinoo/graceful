SUMMARY
Composition line. Two new composition theorems are written with complete proofs and brute-force checked from the README section 1 definitions (0 failures): (A) "core fattening", which generalises README Theorem 13 from gluing at the root to multiplying branches along any subtree containing the root, including gluing 2m+1 copies of any growth-placed tree along its top bracket; (B) "mixed bunch", where the 2m+1 copies may be different trees, equal in pairs. Neither covers symmetric trees: every rule leaves a by-product of m extra edges. A falsifiable Observation: every vertex of S is an anchor of the corona of S, for all 52,994 rooted trees S on at most 14 vertices (not proved). Dead ends recorded with smallest obstructions. Novelty against the literature was not checked.

DEAD ENDS
1. Identify two anchors (v = v'): the glued vertex need not be an anchor. Over all 3,321 unordered pairs of anchored rooted trees with at most 7 vertices each (joinprobe.py), it fails for 21; smallest: 6 vertices (root with a pendant path of 2 edges and a child carrying 2 leaves). Path on 4 from its end, taken twice, gives the middle of the path on 7. All 3,321 composites still have some growth placement.
2. Join two anchors by an edge: v is not an anchor of the composite for 21 of 3,321 pairs, and neither v nor v' is for 8; smallest: path on 3 and path on 4 joined end to end (two middle vertices of the path on 7). All composites still have some growth placement.
3. An even number of copies, or any multiplier other than 2m+1, in the window scheme: for a non-root edge the copy lengths are kc + s(o_child(i) - o_parent(i)), and these offsets sum to 0 over the copies, so the length window must be symmetric, k odd, and the leftover at the root is forced to be exactly m = (k-1)/2 edges. So the by-product S can never be removed: symmetric trees (root with d equal branches and nothing else) are NOT covered by Theorem 13, A or B, except through the already-known towers.
4. Different trees in different copies without the pairing: dropping H0 broke all 27,180 tested composites, violating H1/H2 all 1,816 (m = 1, at most 5 edges).
5. Classical Delta-construction (copies of T on every vertex of S, S-edges multiples of |T|): growth forces the copy at S-vertex i to fit entirely below i's edge towards the root. In block form with T = K_2 this is Lemma C and covers only 8 of 277 anchored rooted trees at s = 9; first obstruction K_{1,3} at its centre. The alpha-twist with a non-monotone copy (lengths (n+1) - c) reverses the order of lengths, so it is not growth unless T is a caterpillar.
6. Not done: a closure count (which trees arise from K_1 by Lemma 6, gluing, Theorems 13, A, B), and any literature check of Theorems A and B; they are product constructions in the Stanton-Zarnke / Koh-Rogers-Tan spirit and novelty is not claimed. ===== CLAIM 0 [proved]
STATEMENT
Theorem A (core fattening). Labels from 0. Let T be a tree with a growth labeling f: V(T) -> {0..n} (n edges), r the vertex labelled 0, z the vertex labelled n (so rz is the top bracket). Let C be a subtree of T containing r, let m >= 0 and k = 2m+1. Every component B of T - C is joined to C by one edge, to a vertex x_B of C.
Case 1 (z not in C): let S be a tree with m edges and a growth labeling on 0..m with root s0. Define T*: take C; glue S on by s0 = r; for every edge of C add 2m new leaves at its end nearer r; for every component B of T - C add 2m+1 disjoint copies of B, each joined to x_B the way B is.
Case 2 (z in C): let S be a tree on 2m+2 vertices with a growth labeling g on 0..2m+1 in which no bracket of length <= m joins a label <= m to a label >= m+1. Define T*: take C; glue S on so that S's top bracket is the edge rz (g^-1(0) = r, g^-1(2m+1) = z); for every edge of C other than rz add 2m leaves at its end nearer r; add 2m+1 copies of every component of T - C as in case 1.
Then T* has a growth placement with r at label 0 (so r is an anchor of T*; in case 2, z carries the top label kn, so z is an anchor too).
C = {r} is README Theorem 13. Corollary (book): if T has a growth placement with top bracket rz, then for every m and every tree G with m edges anchored at its root, the tree made of 2m+1 copies of T glued along the edge rz, plus G glued at r, plus m leaves at r, has a growth placement anchored at r (end piece: G on 0..m, labels m+1..2m joined to 0). Corollary (multiplying at an internal vertex): for any vertex x of a growth-placed T, take C = the path from the anchor r to x; every branch hanging off that path can be multiplied by 2m+1 at the price of 2m leaves per path edge and an end piece at r.

PROOF
Put delta(x) = -x-m for x <= 0 and m+1-x for x > 0 on I = [-m,m]; delta is an involution of I, and tau(x) = x - delta(x) (= 2x+m for x <= 0, 2x-m-1 for x > 0) takes each value of I exactly once. For v outside C let d(v) >= 1 be its distance to C.
Labeling of T*. (i) x in C gets k f(x). (ii) The 2m leaves added for an edge p -> x of C (p nearer r) get k f(x) + j, j in I\{0}, and hang on p. (iii) Copy i in I of a vertex v outside C gets k f(v) + e_i(v), where e_i(v) = i if d(v) is odd and delta(i) if d(v) is even; it is joined to copy i of its parent (to the parent itself if the parent is in C). (iv) Case 1: S keeps its labels 0..m. Case 2: a vertex of S with g <= m keeps g, a vertex with g >= m+1 gets g + kn - 2m - 1.
Labels. For every vertex y of T other than r (and other than z in case 2) the vertices made from y carry exactly the window k f(y) + I: for y in C by (i),(ii); for y outside C because i -> e_i(y) is the identity or delta, a permutation of I. The windows for f(y) = 1..n tile [m+1, kn+m] (case 1) and those for 1..n-1 tile [m+1, kn-m-1] (case 2). S fills [0,m] in case 1 and [0,m] together with [kn-m, kn] in case 2 (r = 0, z = kn). So every label of 0..kn+m (case 1), 0..kn (case 2), is used once.
Lengths. Let e = {p, y} be an edge of T of length c, p nearer r, f(y) = f(p) + s c with s = +-1; in case 2 take e != rz. The edges of T* made from e have exactly the lengths kc + I: if p, y in C they are kc (edge of C) and |k s c + j| = kc + s j, j != 0 (the leaves); if p in C and y not, they are |k s c + i| = kc + s i, i in I; if neither is in C, the offsets of p and y in copy i are E and delta(E) with E in {i, delta(i)}, so the length is |k s c + delta(E) - E| = kc - s tau(E), and E runs over I with i. (All offsets are below k in absolute value, so the signs are as written.) These windows tile [m+1, kn+m] in case 1 and [m+1, kn-m-1] in case 2. S supplies 1..m in case 1. In case 2 the brackets of S of length <= m do not cross the middle by hypothesis and keep their lengths 1..m; the brackets of length >= m+1 must cross it (a half has only m+1 labels) and grow by kn-2m-1 to kn-m..kn. Every length is used once.
Downhill (README Theorem 7). Give each edge of T* a level: c if it was made from a T-edge of length c, n for the stretched brackets of S in case 2, 0 for the short brackets of S. An edge of level c has length in [kc-m, kc+m], and k = 2m+1, so a higher level means a strictly longer edge. Along a path away from r: a step from an edge made from a T-edge to another one drops the level, because the corresponding T-edges are consecutive on a path away from r in T (leaves and copies hang on the image of the parent). Steps inside S go downhill because S is a growth labeling and (iv) preserves the order of lengths of S (long ones all grow by the same amount and stay above the short ones). The only vertices of S that carry anything else are r (the root) and, in case 2, z, whose edge towards r has length kn, the maximum. Short edges of S (level 0) are followed only by edges of S. Hence lengths strictly decrease away from r, and by Theorem 7 this is a growth placement with r at label 0. The tree drawn is T* by construction (i)-(iv). For the book corollary take C = {r, z}; the end piece 'G on 0..m and labels m+1..2m joined to 0, top bracket {0, 2m+1}' is a growth labeling whose brackets of length <= m lie inside [0,m]. QED


===== CLAIM 1 [proved]
STATEMENT
Theorem B (mixed bunch). Labels from 0. Let m >= 0, k = 2m+1, I = [-m, m], delta(x) = -x-m (x <= 0), m+1-x (x > 0); delta is an involution of I with exactly one fixed point. Let S be a tree with m edges and a growth labeling on 0..m rooted at label 0, and for each i in I let T_i be a tree with n edges and a growth labeling on 0..n rooted at label 0 (the trees may differ), such that (H0) all T_i have the same set of labels adjacent to the root; (H1) for every label l, the vertex labelled l has the same depth parity in T_i and in T_delta(i); (H2) for every length c not on a root edge, the lower-labelled end of the bracket of length c has the same depth parity in T_i and T_delta(i). Then the tree obtained by gluing S and all T_i at their roots has a growth placement anchored at the glued root. H1 and H2 hold whenever T_i = T_delta(i) as labelled trees.
Corollary (tree language, no labelings in the hypothesis). Let S be a tree with m edges anchored at s0, and U_0, U_1, ..., U_m trees with the same number of edges, U_j anchored at u_j. Join s0 by new edges to u_0 and to two copies each of u_1, ..., u_m. The result has a growth placement anchored at s0. (README Theorem 13 gives this only when all U_j are equal.) For example, with m = 1: a root with one leaf and pendant subtrees U_0, U_1, U_1.

EVIDENCE
/home/user/graceful/src/attack/composition/mixed_bunch.py, all admissible families of growth labelings generated from the definition, every S: m=1, T_i with at most 6 edges: 5,073 composites, 0 failures; m=2, at most 5 edges: 15,458 composites, 0 failures; m=3, at most 3 edges: 84 composites, 0 failures. Each composite is tested from the definition and compared as a rooted tree with the glued tree built without labels. Pairs of DISTINCT labelings satisfying H1-H2 exist but are rare (63 unordered pairs at m=1 up to 6 edges). teeth.py (m=1, at most 5 edges): dropping H0 breaks 27,180 of 27,180 composites; violating H1/H2 breaks 1,816 of 1,816.

PROOF
Labeling: S keeps 0..m. A non-root vertex v of T_i with label l and depth d gets k l + e_i(v), e_i(v) = i if d is odd, delta(i) if d is even; edges as in T_i, root edges going to the glued root 0. Put tau(x) = x - delta(x); tau is a bijection of I and tau(delta(x)) = -tau(x).
Labels. Fix l in 1..n and let D = {i : label l is at even depth in T_i}. The offsets used at l are {i : i not in D} and {delta(i) : i in D}. By H1, D is delta-invariant, so delta(D) = D and the offsets are exactly I: the copies of l fill the window k l + I. The windows tile [m+1, kn+m]; S fills [0,m].
Lengths. Fix c in 1..n. By H0 the bracket of length c is a root edge in all T_i or in none. If it is, its copy in T_i joins 0 to kc + i: lengths kc + I. If not, in T_i it joins labels a < a+c at consecutive depths, so the two offsets are i and delta(i) in some order and the copy has length kc + (offset of upper end) - (offset of lower end), which is kc + tau(i) if the lower end is at even depth and kc - tau(i) if it is at odd depth (offsets differ by at most 2m < k, so no sign change). Let L = {i : lower end at even depth}; by H2, L and I\L are delta-invariant, so {-tau(i) : i not in L} = {tau(delta(i)) : i not in L} = tau(I\L), and the lengths are kc + tau(I) = kc + I. These windows tile [m+1, kn+m]; S supplies 1..m.
Downhill. Inside a copy of T_i, consecutive edges away from the root have T-lengths c > c', hence composite lengths >= kc - m > kc' + m; S is unchanged and all its edges are shorter than every copy edge, but S hangs only on the root. By README Theorem 7 this is a growth placement anchored at the root.
Corollary: give U_j a growth labeling on 0..q with u_j at 0, reflect it (labels q - x; still a growth labeling by Theorem 7, now with u_j at the top label q), shift by 1 and add a root at 0 joined to u_j: a growth labeling of 'pendant U_j' on 0..q+1 whose root has the single neighbour label q+1 (README Lemma 6). Use it for the fixed point of delta when j = 0 and for both members of the j-th delta-pair when j >= 1 (there are m pairs). H0 holds (root neighbour set {q+1}) and H1, H2 hold because paired labelings are identical. QED


===== CLAIM 2 [verified-only]
STATEMENT
Observation (corona; NOT proved). For a tree S let cor(S) be S with one new leaf attached to every vertex. For every tree S on s <= 14 vertices and EVERY vertex v of S, cor(S) has a growth placement with v at label 0, i.e. every core vertex of a corona is an anchor, including the vertices that are not anchors of S itself (such as the three middle vertices of the path on 7). Falsifiable strengthening proposed: this holds for all trees S.
Accompanying dead end (Lemma C, proved, and why it does not explain the observation): if S has a growth labeling on 0..s-1 with e_i the length of the bracket from i towards the root, and pi is a permutation of 0..s-1 such that the numbers phi(pi(i) - i) (phi(d) = d for d >= 0, -d-1 for d < 0) are all different and phi(pi(i) - i) <= e_i - 1 for every non-root i, then labelling vertex i with 2i and its leaf with 2 pi(i) + 1 is a growth placement of cor(S) anchored at the same root. This block form of the Stanton-Zarnke / Koh-Rogers-Tan construction almost never applies: it covers 2, 2, 4, 4, 4, 8 of the 4, 9, 19, 45, 110, 277 anchored rooted trees for s = 4..9; the smallest uncovered case is the star K_{1,3} rooted at its centre.
